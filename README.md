# 🚀 AeroTutor AI

### Your Personal AI-Powered STEM & Aerospace Tutor

AeroTutor AI is an intelligent Android learning platform that transforms traditional STEM education into an interactive and personalized learning experience.

Instead of simply answering questions, AeroTutor AI helps students:

**Learn → Practice → Test → Analyze → Improve → Experiment**

---

## 🏆 Hackathon Project

AeroTutor AI is a hackathon project focused on using **Generative AI to make STEM education more interactive, accessible, and personalized.**

### The Problem

Students have access to huge amounts of educational content, but learning is often:

- 📚 Static
- 🧠 Difficult to personalize
- 📝 Focused heavily on memorization
- ❌ Poor at identifying individual weaknesses
- 🎙️ Not always accessible through different learning styles
- 🔬 Limited in interactive STEM experimentation

A student may understand one part of physics perfectly while struggling with another, but traditional textbooks do not automatically adapt to that student.

### Our Solution

**AeroTutor AI turns educational resources into an intelligent learning environment.**

It combines:

- 🤖 Generative AI tutoring
- 📖 Educational document interaction
- 🧠 Personalized learning
- 📝 AI-generated quizzes
- 🎯 Adaptive practice
- 📊 Learning analytics
- 🎙️ Voice-oriented learning
- ✈️ Aerospace simulations
- 🔬 STEM experimentation
